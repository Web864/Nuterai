"Shared" means processed by a service provider to operate NutriAI, not sold, used for advertising, or shared for another company's independent use. Do not add claims about analytics, crash reporting, Health Connect, advertising IDs, or payment processors unless they are present in the release build.

Account deletion is available in Settings -> Delete account and at the public deletion page. It removes the Auth account and user-owned data through cleanup and database cascades. Limited de-identified audit information may be retained where necessary for security or legal compliance.
