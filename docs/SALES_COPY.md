# NutriAI sales copy and listings

Every claim below is something the product does today (checked against the
code and a local end-to-end run on 26 Nov 2026). The live website copy is in
`src/content/marketing.ts`; this file is for store listings, launch posts and
ads. Rules: general wellness only, no medical claims, no promised results, no
user counts or ratings until you have real ones.

## Positioning

**One line:** NutriAI is the AI calorie tracker and coach for the food you
actually eat, desi meals included.

**Who it's for:** adults who want to lose weight, build muscle or eat better,
especially people whose meals (roti, daal, biryani, karahi) don't fit
Western-only calorie apps.

## Headline options

1. Count calories in the food you actually eat.
2. Snap it, scan it or just type it. NutriAI does the counting.
3. Your AI nutrition coach, fluent in roti and daal.

## Subhead

Personal calorie and macro targets, photo, barcode and plain-words food logging,
an AI coach that knows your goals and today's log, and workout plans for your
equipment. Free to start, no credit card.

## Buyer benefits

- **Log a meal in seconds.** Take a photo, scan a barcode, or type "2 rotis and
  daal". Review the estimate, fix anything, save.
- **Built for desi food.** Everyday South Asian meals are understood, not
  forced into a Western food database.
- **Know your numbers.** Daily calorie, protein, carb, fat, fiber and water
  targets calculated from your body, activity and goal.
- **A coach that knows you.** Ask what to eat for dinner, how to hit your
  protein, or for a full day of meals. It uses your targets, diet preference
  (halal, vegetarian, keto and more) and allergies.
- **Workouts that fit your setup.** AI plans for a full gym, home gym, basic
  equipment or no equipment, with an exercise library and session logging.
- **Stay consistent.** Reminders, streaks, badges, water, sleep, weight and
  mood tracking, and an optional community feed.
- **Your data stays yours.** Download everything any time, delete your account
  in the app or on the web.

## Feature list

- Calorie and macro targets (Mifflin-St Jeor)
- AI photo food logging with review and edit
- Barcode scanner (Open Food Facts)
- Plain-text meal logging
- AI Coach chat with your profile and recent logs as context
- Meal and full-day meal plans on request in the Coach
- AI workout plans for 4 equipment levels, exercise library, session logger
- Water, sleep, weight and mood tracking
- Reminders (web notifications and Android local notifications)
- XP, streaks, badges, leaderboards, community feed
- Light and dark theme
- Data export (JSON) and account deletion

## FAQ (short versions for listings)

- **Is it free?** Yes. Free has no time limit and needs no card. Pro (planned)
  raises daily AI limits.
- **How accurate are photo estimates?** They are estimates. You always see and
  can edit the result before it's saved.
- **Does it work for Pakistani and Indian food?** Yes, that is a core focus.
- **Is it medical advice?** No. NutriAI gives general wellness guidance. Speak
  to a doctor or dietitian about a health condition.
- **Who can use it?** Adults aged 18 and over.
